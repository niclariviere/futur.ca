The problem

You want an agent to act. You don't trust it to.

If you run a business of five to fifty people, you already have the repeating work an agent could take: quotes, follow-ups, scheduling, reporting, the inbox.

The demos are impressive. The question that stops you is simpler: what happens the first time it sends the wrong thing to the wrong customer?

That is the right question. Trust is the product, not the model.

How it works

Autonomy is earned, one rung at a time.

I run my own agents this way, with my own money at risk. The same ladder applies to yours.

Paper

The agent reads your systems and proposes each action. You approve or reject from Slack, Telegram or WhatsApp. Nothing leaves without you.

Every engagement starts here.

Supervised

The agent acts within written limits: amounts, recipients, hours. Every action is logged and you get a daily digest.

Promotion happens on evidence, not on a demo.

Autonomous

The agent runs. You keep the ledger, the limits and a panic switch that stops everything in one message.

Demotion is one rung down, never a debate.

The agent

Connected to the tools you already use: email, calendar, CRM, accounting, chat. Through open protocols such as MCP, so nothing is locked to me.

The ledger

Every proposal, action, hold and promotion, in plain language, in a place you can read. It is what makes the ladder real.

The operator

I watch it, tune it and answer for it. You reach me in the same channel the agent uses.

FUTUR is the company that holds what I build. Client work funds the ventures and keeps the tooling sharp.

I take on a small number of engagements at a time, in French or English, from Québec. You work with me directly, from the first conversation to the ledger.

A good fit if

  • You run an owner-led business of roughly 5 to 50 people.
  • Your team already talks in Slack, Teams, Telegram or WhatsApp.
  • You have at least one process you would hand off tomorrow if you trusted the hands.

Not a fit if

  • You want a chatbot on your homepage and nothing more.
  • You want full autonomy on day one. It is earned here.
  • You need a large team or a fixed-price enterprise contract.
